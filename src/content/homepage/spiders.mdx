It's time we face the facts: there aren't enough spiders on the world wide web.

In October, 2026, for a talk at [WDC](https://webdevconf.com/) in Bristol, I built https://spiders.duncanpetrie.com, which is a place where you can be a spider. Bring a friend! You can be spiders together.

It's also a [browser extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/a-spider-on-the-web/jnlhooedjlgccjkicbbolgbojlaigkle?authuser=0&hl=en)!
